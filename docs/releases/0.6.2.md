- The canvas, notes, PDFs, the browser and Ask Knot are now tabs in up to two columns beside the sidebar. Move, separate or close any of them; the top bar is gone to give your notes more room.
- Page view opens a note in its own tab beside the canvas (Ctrl+Enter on a selected card), with the same floating table of contents. Note tabs remember where you scrolled.
- Column headers are simple: each column has its tabs and a + button, Undo and Redo appear once, and a tab's right-click menu moves, swaps or closes it. A count such as +3 shows tabs that don't fit.
- Focus (or Ctrl+Shift+Enter) fills the window with the column you're working in, marked by a faint blue tint; the other column becomes a slim rail. Esc brings both back.
- Search (Ctrl+K), Ask Knot and Back/Forward work with tabs: insert into the note you're writing, jump to matches inside note tabs, and step between tabs.
- Each notebook's PDF reader reopens at the PDF, zoom and position you left it at.
- Export is now a dialog: pick Markdown, PNG or PDF and save.
- Settings → General → Sidebar puts the sidebar on the left or right of the window.
