- Share a notebook as one file: open the notebook's menu and choose Share as file…. Notebooks shown on its canvas come along, and you can tick linked ones too.
- Open a .knotbook file by double-clicking it, or use Import… in the Library. You get your own editable copy.
- If you already have that notebook, choose Keep both or Replace mine. Knot makes a backup before replacing.
- Notebooks the sender left out show as "Not shared", and fill in by themselves if you import them later.
- Closing Knot while it's minimized now saves and closes normally.
- Canvas link tiles stay small and line up with their selection box.
