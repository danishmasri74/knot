- Ask Knot understands your canvas: which cards are in which frame, what your arrows connect and roughly where things are. Ask "what depends on this?", "walk me through this frame" or "check my map".
- New canvas cards are laid out by how they connect (steps left to right, "part of" as a tree, a central idea in the middle) and can link to cards already there. "Tidy up this frame" rearranges existing cards, and arrow labels now show.
- "Break this into ideas?" replaces a long card with one-idea cards in a frame, with coloured arrows between them. Anything left out is kept on an "Other details" card, and Undo brings the original back.
- Ask Knot can suggest missing connections, each with a reason, and look at your canvas when you ask about drawings or colours. The image is made on your computer and isn't saved.
- Ask Knot makes multiple-choice quizzes on a card beside the note or frame (Settings → AI → Make quizzes). Show on canvas jumps to anything Ask Knot made.
- PowerPoint decks (.pptx) open like PDFs, using your own PowerPoint or LibreOffice: read slides, highlight, search and ask about them.
- The Library is a tab with its own columns. Ask Knot reads the page behind a selected website card, and its links follow Settings → Web links.
- Fixed: Ask Knot sometimes said it couldn't cite notes that were right there, and mixed other canvas pictures into questions about a selected PDF.
