# Unreleased

- Word documents (.docx) open like PDFs, using your own Word or LibreOffice: read pages, highlight, search and ask about them. Tracked changes show as the final text, and Open in Word opens the original.
- The PDF reader header fits on one row: the file name, page, zoom, Open in Word or PowerPoint, and Ask Knot, with what Ask Knot reads picked from its arrow.
- The collapsed sidebar has Library and Settings buttons, with Settings at the bottom showing a dot when an update is ready.
- Card Deck fits as many columns as the panel allows, cards on the current canvas are tinted green, each card shows its placement in colour with an Add button, and one Filter and sort menu covers placement, card type and order.
- When an Ask Knot answer suits it, buttons under the answer offer next steps such as canvas cards, a mind map, a quiz or a new note. Each one shows a preview before anything changes.
- Cards Ask Knot adds to a canvas leave room for their arrow labels, so labels no longer sit on top of the cards, and comparisons ("A vs. B") are no longer laid out as a step-by-step flow.
- Cards added to a canvas by Ask Knot or Add note no longer land on top of PDFs, images or drawings and hide them.
- Select up to three PDFs or slide decks on a canvas and Ask Knot reads all of them, not just the first. The chip shows how many files are included.
- In answers about PDFs and slide decks, citations like "slide 3" are links that open the file at that page. With several files, each citation names its file.
- Next-step buttons also appear under answers about PDFs and slide decks, and they build on the answer without sending the files again.
