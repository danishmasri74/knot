# Unreleased
- Ask Knot answers show tables, nested lists, checklists, quotes, links and code blocks with a Copy button. Add reply as one card keeps them as real headings, lists and tables, and Copy keeps tables when pasting into Word or email.
- While Ask Knot works you see each step, such as reading files or searching your notes, then a one-line summary above the answer.
- Ask Knot to quiz you and the questions appear right in the answer: pick an option to check it, see your score, try again, or save it as a quiz card in Card Deck.
- Hover a slide citation in Ask Knot to preview that slide, and see the cited slides as thumbnails under the answer.
- Long Ask Knot answers with many sections can fold, and Cover answers hides every answer so you can test your recall before opening each one.
- New Study tab (column + menu): turn up to three PDFs or slide decks into a study course with a progress tracker, short lessons, MCQ checkpoints graded after you submit, a final mixed exam, readiness per topic and a revision sheet you can save to the canvas. Progress is saved in the notebook.
- Make a study course straight from the canvas toolbar when PDFs or slide decks are selected, or from the suggestion under an Ask Knot answer.
- Switch between Ask Knot and Study from the top of either panel; the other tab opens in the same column if it was closed. Ask Knot suggests Study when a chat keeps coming back to the same slides or asks about exam prep, and you can dismiss it for that chat.
