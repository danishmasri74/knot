# Unreleased

- The mini browser stays visible when a menu opens elsewhere on screen; it only hides while a menu covers it.
- The canvas is now a tab like the others: move it to any column, put another tab in front of it, or close it, with two columns beside the sidebar.
- Note tabs you have not looked at for a while unload their editor, so having many notes open stays fast. They come back with your edits and scroll position.
- Page view now opens the note in its own tab beside the canvas, so you can read a note and see the canvas at the same time. Ctrl+Enter opens the selected card this way.
- One Focus button at the right end of the column headers (or Ctrl+Shift+Enter) fills the window with the column you're working in; the other column shrinks to a slim rail. Click the rail to switch columns, or press Focus or Esc to see both again. Ask Knot's "Open as page" now focuses its tab.
- The top bar is gone, giving its space to your notes. The notebook's name, star, save state and actions sit at the start of the first column, each column has a + menu to open tabs, and back, forward and the sidebar toggle moved into the sidebar.
- Settings → General → Sidebar puts the sidebar on the left or right of the window.
- Note tabs remember where you scrolled, even after restarting Knot.
- On a narrow window, columns that do not fit shrink to rails instead of merging into one.
- Each notebook's PDF reader comes back at the PDF, zoom and position you left it at.
- Back and Forward also step between the tabs you switched to in a notebook.
- Expanding, restoring and rearranging columns now animate smoothly.
- Export is now a dialog: pick Markdown, PNG or PDF and save.
- Column headers are simpler: every column has just its tabs and a + button, Undo and Redo appear once, and moving, separating, swapping or closing tabs is in a tab's right-click menu (or Shift+F10).
- When a column has more tabs than fit, a count such as +3 shows how many are out of view and lists every tab in the column.
- Note tabs show the same floating table of contents as the canvas, and no longer have a Multiple choice bar above the note (type / in a note to add one).
- The "Opening Canvas…" screen covers only the canvas column, right under its tabs, instead of blanking every column and leaving a strip of the old page showing.
- Search (Ctrl+K) works with note tabs: it can insert a found card into the note you were writing in, and it scrolls to and highlights the match inside a note tab.
- Adding a found card to the canvas, or jumping to a card from a PDF, brings the Canvas tab forward so you see it.
- While a column is focused, opening a note or tool brings the columns back instead of covering the canvas.
- Esc shows both columns again after focusing one, wherever you are (except while typing).
- Ask Knot follows the note tab you're working in, so it offers to insert answers into that note.
