# Unreleased

<!-- One line per change a user would notice. Moved into docs/releases/<version>.md on release. -->

- An empty Video or Browser panel now offers to continue the last video (from where you stopped) or web page you viewed.
- YouTube videos, links, PDFs and other files, and images on a canvas are now saved to the Card Deck, with no duplicates: the same video, page or file is one card. PDF cards preview their first page.
- The Card Deck no longer lists empty cards; a card appears once it has content. Leftover empty cards no canvas uses are moved to Trash once.
- The Library's Card Deck lists cards that aren't on any canvas in their own section at the bottom.
- Select several cards in the Library with Ctrl- or Shift-click and press Delete to move them all to Trash.
- Deleting the last copy of an empty or very short note now moves its card to Trash (Undo brings it back), and cards from notebooks in Trash stay out of the Card Deck until restored.
- In Ctrl+K search, press Ctrl+Enter on a note to add its card to the open canvas, or to insert it into the note you're writing.
- Cards can be reused inside notes in Page view: they stay in sync with the original, and "Edit card" changes it everywhere.
