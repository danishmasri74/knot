# Unreleased

<!-- One line per change a user would notice. Moved into docs/releases/<version>.md on release. -->
- Ask Knot no longer needs a mode: it answers, or suggests a rewrite, an addition or canvas cards when you ask for one. Choose what it may suggest under Settings → AI → Abilities or the sparkles button.
- When your OpenAI account runs out of credits, Ask Knot now says so instead of showing a generic interrupted-response error.
- Ask Knot searches your workspace only when a question needs it, writes its own search, and shows what it searched. The Source scope menu is gone; search can be turned off per chat from the sparkles button.
- Open source in Ask Knot now opens the cited card in the view you use (canvas or page) and jumps to the cited words, instead of always switching to page view.
