- Ask Knot answers show tables, nested lists, checklists, quotes, links and code blocks with a Copy button. Add reply as one card keeps them as real headings, lists and tables, and Copy keeps tables when pasting into Word or email.
- While Ask Knot works you see each step, such as reading files or searching your notes, then a one-line summary above the answer.
- Ask Knot to quiz you and the questions appear right in the answer: pick an option to check it, see your score, try again, or save it as a quiz card in Card Deck.
- Hover a slide citation in Ask Knot to preview that slide, and see the cited slides as thumbnails under the answer.
- Long Ask Knot answers with many sections can fold, and Cover answers hides every answer so you can test your recall before opening each one.

- Make a study course straight from the canvas toolbar when PDFs or slide decks are selected, or from the suggestion under an Ask Knot answer.

- Ctrl+click chats in Ask Knot's chat history to select several and delete them together. Press Esc or × to clear the selection.
- Ask Knot composes the canvas instead of laying cards out in a grid: it groups content into framed sections, adds headings, diagram shapes, mind maps and arrows (including to mind map branches and existing cards), and places them where it describes, next to what you asked about. - Ask Knot handles big canvas requests, such as atomic notes for a whole course, in sections: it shows the plan with an estimate, builds each section from its sources, links the sections and checks the layout. Stop keeps what's built, and Continue finishes the rest.
- New canvas content lands in free space next to what you're working on (your selection, a PDF, or what's on screen) instead of far to the right.
- Asking for atomic notes, flashcards or revision notes while a canvas is open puts them on the canvas instead of answering with a wall of text.
- Suggested buttons under an answer use your own words and build from the original sources, not just the answer.
- Break this into ideas lays out the new cards with structure and arrows, in a frame where the card was.
- With slide decks selected on the canvas, Ask Knot can turn them into canvas cards, notes or quizzes instead of only answering in chat.
