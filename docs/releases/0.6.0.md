- Choose whether Knot opens the Library or reopens your last notebook at startup.
- YouTube videos, links, PDFs, files, and images on a canvas are saved to the Card Deck without duplicates. PDF cards preview their first page.
- Website cards show titles, descriptions, and thumbnails on the canvas and in the Card Deck.
- Reuse cards inside notes in Page view, or press Ctrl+Enter in search to add a card to your open canvas or note. Reused cards stay in sync.
- See where a card is used and jump to each location. Cards that aren't on a canvas have their own section in the Library.
- Select multiple cards with Ctrl- or Shift-click and press Delete to move them to Trash. Empty cards stay out of the deck, and unused blank or very short notes move to Trash when their last copy is deleted.
- Double-click a YouTube card to play it in the Video panel. Empty Video and Browser panels offer to resume your last video or page.
- Knot upgrades your workspace when you open it. Older versions cannot open the upgraded workspace.
