- Ask Knot no longer needs a mode: it answers, or suggests a rewrite, an addition or canvas cards when you ask for one.
- Choose what Ask Knot may do on its own in Settings → AI → Abilities, or for one chat from the sparkles button.
- Ask Knot searches your workspace only when a question needs it and shows what it searched. The Source scope menu is gone.
- Ask Knot can search the web (off by default, about $0.01 per search) and look at images in your note when a question is about them. Cited links open in your browser.
- Ask Knot can propose a whole new note in the open notebook (Settings → AI → Create new notes, off by default).
- Open source jumps to the cited words in the view you use, canvas or page.
- Ask Knot in the sidebar opens as a panel beside your note or canvas, so you keep your place.
- Ask Knot tells you when your OpenAI account is out of credits, and a partly read website no longer shows a "partial" label.
