- Word documents (.docx) open like PDFs, using your own Word or LibreOffice: read, highlight, search and ask about them.
- Ask Knot suggests next steps under answers that suit one, such as canvas cards, a mind map, a quiz or a new note, each previewed before anything changes.
- Select up to three PDFs or slide decks and Ask Knot reads them all. Citations like "slide 3" open the file at that page.
- Cards added to a canvas no longer cover PDFs, images or drawings, leave room for arrow labels, and come into view after you apply them.
- Tidy tangled arrows with reroute points: Alt+click an arrow to add a dot you can drag or share between arrows.
- Arrow labels are easier to type, use the canvas font and stay readable on a dark canvas.
- Card Deck fits more columns, tints cards on the current canvas green and has one Filter and sort menu. A new setting can clear out blank unplaced cards.
- The collapsed sidebar has Library and Settings, the PDF reader header fits on one row, and Settings pages are grouped into sections.
